How to Set Up Favourite Groups in TiviMate

Favourite groups let you collect your most-watched channels in one place. You can create multiple groups, like "Sports" or "News", and add channels from any list. Follow these steps on your remote control.

  1. Open the app and go to your playlist. Press the Menu button on your remote. Select "Settings > Playlist". Choose your IPTV subscription (e.g., the one from SubTV IP).
  2. Create a new favourite group. In the playlist view, press the left arrow button to open the sidebar. Scroll down to "Favourites" and press OK. Then press the Menu button again and choose "Add new group". Type a name (e.g., "My Top 20") and press OK.
  3. Add channels to your group. Go back to the full channel list. Highlight a channel you want to add. Press and hold the OK button for 2 seconds. Select "Add to Favourites". Then choose the group you just created. Repeat for each channel.
  4. Organize channels inside a group. Open the Favourites sidebar. Select your group and press OK. Highlight a channel, press the Menu button, and choose "Move". Use the up/down arrows to reorder it, then press OK.
  5. Edit or delete a group. In the Favourites sidebar, highlight the group name (not a channel). Press the Menu button. You can choose "Rename group" or "Delete group". Deleting a group removes only the group, not the channels from your main list.
  6. Switch between groups quickly. While watching any channel, press the down arrow button. This opens the channel list. Press the left arrow to see the sidebar. Here you can jump from one favourite group to another without leaving the screen.

If a Step Fails

  • No favourite groups appear? Make sure your TiviMate is version 5.x or newer. Restart the app. If the issue continues, check that your subscription is active. Contact SubTV IP support via WhatsApp.
  • Channels don’t load in a group? Your internet speed may be low. Set your DNS to Cloudflare (1.1.1.1) and the buffer/cache to 5,000 ms in "Settings > Network > DNS" and "Settings > Playback > Buffer size".
  • Remote buttons don't respond? Try using the TiviMate companion app on your phone for the same steps. Also ensure your device’s firmware is up to date.
  • Group was deleted by accident? You cannot recover a deleted group. You must create a new one and add channels again. Keep a backup of your channel list by using the "Export" option in "Settings > Playlist > Export".

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a favourite group in TiviMate?

Open the app, go to your playlist, press the left arrow to open the sidebar. Scroll to "Favourites", press Menu, choose "Add new group", type a name, and press OK. Then add channels by holding OK on a channel and selecting "Add to Favourites".

Can I rename a favourite group after I create it?

Yes. In the Favourites sidebar, highlight the group name (not a channel). Press the Menu button and select "Rename group". Type the new name and press OK.

Why can't I see my favourite groups on my Samsung TV?

Make sure you are using TiviMate 5.x and that your subscription is active. Go to "Settings > Playlist" and check that your playlist is loaded. If you use a Samsung Smart TV (Tizen), confirm the app is installed from the official store.

How many favourite groups can I make?

There is no fixed limit. You can create as many groups as you want. However, having too many may slow down the sidebar. Keep your groups under 20 for smooth use.

Can I share my favourite groups with another device?

Yes. Use the "Export" feature in "Settings > Playlist > Export" to save a file. Copy that file to the other device, then import it using "Settings > Playlist > Import". Your favourite groups will appear on the new device.

Do favourite groups work with any IPTV subscription?

Yes, the favourite groups feature works with any playlist loaded in TiviMate, including premium subscriptions like SubTV IP. The feature is part of the app, not the service.

What happens to my favourite groups if I change my subscription?

Your favourite groups are saved in TiviMate’s data, not in your subscription. When you load a new playlist, the groups will still exist but may be empty if channel names differ. You can add the new channels to the same groups manually.
